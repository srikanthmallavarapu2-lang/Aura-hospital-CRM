INFRASTRUCTURE FOR PRECISION

Diagnostic & Surgical Infrastructure

Equipped with cutting-edge medical hardware to minimise invasiveness and hospital stay.

1. Biplane Neuro Angiography

Simultaneous dual-plane 3D imaging for instant coiling of cerebral aneurysms and mechanical thrombectomy.

2. Mako & Da Vinci Robotic Platforms

Sub-millimetre surgical accuracy with 3D high-magnification vision, preserving healthy tissue.

3. 3-Tesla Silent MRI

Acoustic reduction technology with wide 70cm bore, delivering neuro and musculoskeletal scans in serene comfort.

4. Molecular Pathology & NGS

On-site next-generation sequencing for targeted cancer chemotherapy and pharmacogenomic drug safety.

5. Hybrid Cardiac Operating Suite

Combines surgical sterility with digital fluoroscopy for seamless conversion during transcatheter procedures.

6. Automated Pneumatic Transport

Hospital-wide vacuum tube network transferring blood samples and urgent drugs between floors in under 30 seconds.
